# Benchmark Registry and Rules

## Programme-wide rules

- Predeclare primary metrics and statistical comparisons.
- Give comparable methods comparable tuning and compute budgets.
- Record package versions, configurations, seeds, runtime, peak memory, and
  failures.
- Preserve raw results; derive tables and figures through scripted analysis.
- Report repeated-seed variation or confidence intervals where applicable.
