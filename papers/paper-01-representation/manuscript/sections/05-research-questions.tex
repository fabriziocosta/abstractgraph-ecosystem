\section{Research Questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}

The mapped construction makes structural traceability part of the
representation, while permutation equivariance is required of admissible
structural operators. Software and serialization tests check that the
implementation respects these definitions; they are not empirical questions
in this paper.

The study instead addresses two questions.

\paragraph{RQ1: Structural discrimination.}
Can faithful Abstract Graph expressions expose and localize structural
distinctions that their atomic components and a lossless concatenation of
component feature vectors do not? Intrinsic discrimination provides the first
answer. Atomic, concatenated, and graph-composed controls isolate the effect of
composition; mapped witnesses identify the responsible base structures.
Pooling, probe, and hash-width ablations locate later information loss.

\paragraph{RQ2: Discrimination--complexity trade-off.}
What computational cost is required for each factor-specific discrimination
gain, and do graph-valued compositions occupy frontier points unavailable to
their atomic and concatenated controls?
