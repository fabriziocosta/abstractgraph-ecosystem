\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This question cannot yet be answered responsibly: the experiments have been
specified but their artifacts are not frozen. The results will first establish
that non-semantic node permutations leave the representation unchanged. They
will then ask which controlled structural changes alter each expression's
signature, whether simple models can use those changes, and whether pooling or
bounded hashing erases them. The final comparison will place these distinctions
against their runtime, memory, and representation-size costs. Expressions that
lose distinctions or lie below the Pareto frontier remain informative and will
not be omitted.
